Prophet Muhammad ﷺ — The Perfect Family Head

مُحَمَّدٌ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ ﷺ

Born: 12 Rabi' al-Awwal, 570 CE, Makkah  |  Passed: 12 Rabi' al-Awwal, 632 CE, Madinah

The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ was not only the final messenger of Allah and the leader of nations — he was, in his home, the most loving, gentle, and exemplary husband and father humanity has ever known. He helped with household chores, mended his own clothes, and said: "The best of you is the one who is best to his family — and I am the best of you to my family." (Sunan al-Tirmidhi)

His relationship with Hazrat Khadijah (RA) was one of profound love and devotion. With Hazrat Aisha (RA), he was playful and tender. With his daughter Hazrat Fatima (RA), he would stand and kiss her forehead whenever she entered. He said of his grandsons Hasan and Husayn: "They are my fragrance in this world."

"The best of you is the one who is best to his family — and I am the best of you to my family."— Prophet Muhammad ﷺ (Sunan al-Tirmidhi, Hadith 3895 — Sahih)

Hazrat Fatima al-Zahra رضي الله عنها

فاطمة الزهراء بنت محمد رسول الله ﷺ

Born: c. 605 CE, Makkah  |  Passed: c. 632 CE, Madinah — approximately 90 days after her father ﷺ

Hazrat Fatima (RA) — honoured with the title al-Zahra (the Radiant) and Sayyidat Nisa' al-'Alamin (Leader of the Women of all the Worlds) — was the youngest and most beloved daughter of Prophet Muhammad ﷺ. When a hostile mob poured filth upon her father during prayer, young Fatima rushed to him — such that the Prophet ﷺ called her Umm Abiha (Mother of her Father). The Prophet ﷺ declared her one of the four greatest women in all of history.

She married Hazrat Ali (RA) in the first year of Hijra, and is the mother of Hasan and Husayn (RA) — the beloved grandsons of the Prophet ﷺ. She passed away approximately ninety days after her father ﷺ.

"Fatima is a part of me. Whoever angers her has angered me, and whoever pleases her has pleased me."— Prophet Muhammad ﷺ (Sahih al-Bukhari, Book 62, Hadith 74)

Hazrat Ali ibn Abi Talib رضي الله عنه

عليّ بن أبي طالب كرم الله وجهه

Born: c. 600 CE (13 Rajab), Makkah  |  Martyred: 21 Ramadan, 661 CE, Kufa

Hazrat Ali (RA) — cousin and son-in-law of Prophet Muhammad ﷺ and the fourth Rightly Guided Caliph — is among the most revered figures in Islamic history. Raised in the Prophet's own household, he was among the earliest Muslims and is among the ten Companions promised Paradise. On the night of the Hijra, he lay in the Prophet's bed as a decoy — offering his life for Islam.

He fought in every major battle alongside the Prophet ﷺ. His wisdom — collected in Nahj al-Balagha (Peak of Eloquence) — is among the most profound works in Arabic literature after the Quran and Hadith. He was martyred in 661 CE while prostrating in Fajr prayer.

"Your remedy is within you, but you do not sense it. Your sickness is from you, but you do not perceive it. You presume you are a small entity, but within you is enfolded the entire universe."— Hazrat Ali ibn Abi Talib رضي الله عنه (Nahj al-Balagha)
