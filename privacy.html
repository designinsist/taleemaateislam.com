Privacy Policy

Building on a trusted Sunni scholarly legacy, Taleemaat-e-Islam is committed to authenticity, clarity, and transparency in how we publish and operate this website.

Last Updated: 09 April 2026

🕌 Who We Are

Taleemaat-e-Islam (taleemaateislam.com) builds on the legacy of Sheikh Ahmad Sirhindi (Mujaddid Alf Thani, 1564-1624) and Shah Waliullah Dehlawi (1703-1762). We provide authoritative content aligned with Ahl-us-Sunnat wal-Jama'at beliefs, including authentic Quranic commentary (translation with brief tafseer) and sacred texts designed to refine the inner self and purify the heart.

Our platform offers Dars-e-Quran audio, free Islamic books in Urdu and English, daily Namaz timings, Hijri calendar, Qibla direction, and spiritual resources as a non-commercial public service to strengthen the ultimate bond with Allah Almighty and His Prophet Hazrat Muhammad (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him).

This website is operated as a non-commercial, educational resource. We do not sell products, require account registration, or collect payment information of any kind.

📋 Information We Collect

We collect minimal information. Specifically:

  • Analytics data — via Google Analytics (GA4), we collect anonymised information such as pages visited, time on site, device type, and approximate location (country/city level). No personally identifiable information is captured.
  • City selection — when you select your city on our Prayer Times page, this preference is used only to fetch prayer times via the Aladhan API. It is not stored by us.
  • Email enquiries — if you contact us by email, we receive only what you choose to send us.

We do not collect your name, address, phone number, or any form of personal identification through this website.

🍪 Cookies & Third-Party Services

This website uses the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Google Analytics — tracks anonymised usage statistics to help us improve the website. You can opt out via Google's opt-out tool.
  • Google Fonts — loads the Nunito, Playfair Display, Amiri, and Noto Nastaliq Urdu typefaces. Google may log font requests.
  • Aladhan API — provides prayer times, Hijri calendar data, and Qibla direction. Your selected city is sent to their servers to retrieve timings.
  • Google Drive / YouTube — Islamic books are embedded via Google Drive previews; the Dars-e-Quran Short on the homepage is served by YouTube. Both services may set their own cookies.

We do not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or any retargeting technology.

📊 How We Use Information

The anonymised analytics data we collect is used solely to:

  • Understand which content is most beneficial to our visitors
  • Improve the website experience and performance
  • Identify technical issues across devices and browsers

We do not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties for commercial purposes.

📚 Free Books & Downloads

All Islamic books available on this website — including Khuda Mojood Hai, Namaz with English and Urdu Translation, Meet God, Islam & Science, Integration of Islam with Science, Yaqeen Ka Safar, Prayer & Supplication, and Fallacy of Evolution — are provided free of charge for educational and spiritual benefit.

Books are hosted on Google Drive. Downloading or reading these books does not require you to provide us with any personal information. Google Drive's own privacy policy applies when accessing these files.

Our website links to external platforms including YouTube, Facebook, TikTok, WhatsApp, and Spotify for our social channels. Once you leave taleemaateislam.com, this Privacy Policy no longer applies. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any external site you visit.
